# DESIGN.md — BCZ Store

Visual direction for the BCZ Store web app. Agents must follow this file when building or editing UI. antislop filters generic AI patterns; this file supplies the actual direction.

## Product

Digital Roblox shop: game accounts, items, and related services. Buyers are Indonesian teens and young adults who already live in Discord / Roblox UI. Trust and speed matter more than marketing polish.

## Personality

- **Direct and slightly sharp** — no corporate soft-speak, no “unlock the power of”
- **Game-native** — denser information than a SaaS landing page; feels closer to a client UI than a brochure
- **Calm confidence** — glass and depth, not neon chaos or purple SaaS gradients

## Mood keywords

glass · night storefront · catalog density · quick scan · low drama

## Color

| Role | Light | Dark | Notes |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| Page bg | `#eef2f7` | `#020617` | Cool slate |
| Accent | `#0284c7` | `#38bdf8` | Sky — primary actions |
| VIP | `#b45309` | `#fbbf24` | VIP price only |

**Do not use** generic blue→purple or purple→pink hero gradients.

## Layout

- App shell: top navbar + glass sidebar (collapsible) + footer
- Catalog: dense product grid; filter chips near top
- Mobile: always-mounted drawer, translateX, 44px+ taps

## Liveliness dials

- ENERGY: 2 · RHYTHM: 2 · MOTION: 2
